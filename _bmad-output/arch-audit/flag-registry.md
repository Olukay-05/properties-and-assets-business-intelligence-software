# Architecture Audit Flag Registry

This is the authoritative record of architectural flags, their owners,
resolution status, and where the resolution lives. Never delete a flag; update
its status and add a Resolution Log entry.

## Status key

- 🔴 OPEN-P0 — blocks the next handoff.
- 🟡 OPEN-P1 — travels forward as a mandatory constraint.
- 🔵 OPEN-P2 — travels forward; resolve before architecture is finalised or at designated gate.
- ✅ RESOLVED — a resolution is written in the referenced artifact.
- ⏸️ DEFERRED — deliberately postponed with rationale and target phase.
